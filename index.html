Optimization

Evolutionary computation for optimizing distribution systems

A genetic search system that finds resilient, lower-cost distribution layouts across complex operational constraints.

AIOptimizationPython

Goal

Minimize delivery cost by relocating assets to distribution points.

Challenges

  • A large permutation search space makes exact methods impractical on realistic instances
  • Individuals have permutable characteristics
  • Relocations must remain realistically constrained

Approach

  • Stochastic approximation with a genetic algorithm
  • Initialization, crossover and mutation operators adapted to the problem constraints

Results

  • Three times lower delivery cost than traditional methods while respecting relocation constraints
  • A robust, effective and flexible method for resource allocation

NoSQL Graph Analytics for E-commerce Behavior

Mapping customer-product relationships to reveal purchasing patterns that tables tend to hide.

Neo4jGraph DataAnalytics

Goal

Model user-product interactions to answer behavioral questions about funnels, related purchases and price dynamics, informing sales and inventory strategy.

Challenges

  • User journeys do not map cleanly to relational schemas
  • Session context and timestamps must remain accurate for funnels and orders

Approach

  • Neo4j graph model
  • Analytical Cypher queries

Results

  • Built a graph spanning users, sessions, products, brands and categories
  • Delivered funnel, basket, price and session insights
  • Indexes improved query plans and reduced database work
